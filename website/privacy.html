Privacy policy.

Effective 9 October 2026. Applies to the ElementLens browser extension, published by JupiterNexa.

Summary

ElementLens does not collect, store on any server, sell or share any personal data. It has no account, no analytics and no tracking. Everything it does happens locally in your browser.

What the extension reads

When you click the ElementLens icon (or press the shortcut), it gets access to the current tab only. It reads the page's content and styles to show you the HTML, CSS and other information about the element you pick. This information stays in your browser. It is only copied to your clipboard or saved as a file when you press a Copy or Download button.

ElementLens cannot see your other tabs, your browsing history, or any page you did not open it on.

What is stored, and where

DataWherePurpose
Your settings (formats, theme, open sections) Browser extension storage (sync) Remember your preferences. If you use browser sync, your browser may sync these settings to your other devices through your browser account.
Panel position and width Browser extension storage (this device only) Reopen the panel where you left it.

No page content, copied code or browsing data is ever stored by the extension.

Network requests

The extension itself sends no data anywhere. The Assets section shows thumbnails of images that the page already uses; your browser loads those from the same addresses the page uses. The "Open" button opens such an image in a new tab.

Permissions

PermissionWhy it is needed
activeTabAccess the current tab, only after you click the icon or use the shortcut.

ElementLens requests no access to all websites and runs no code from remote servers.

Third parties

None. ElementLens uses no analytics, advertising or third-party services. This website uses no cookies or trackers either.

Children

ElementLens is a developer tool and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new effective date.
